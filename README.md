# Oriel

A new wallpaper, on your schedule. Oriel is a free Mac app that sets a fresh, high-quality Unsplash
photo as your desktop wallpaper, the same photo on every device of your account.

**[Download Oriel for Mac](https://github.com/oriel-releases/macos/releases/latest/download/Oriel.dmg)** · [Website](https://oriel.akbarjon.uz)

This repository hosts Oriel's releases. Installed copies update themselves from here.

Requires macOS 26 Tahoe or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. Oriel is in beta and not notarized by Apple:
open the disk image, drag Oriel to Applications and open it. When macOS says it can't verify Oriel,
click **Done**, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
